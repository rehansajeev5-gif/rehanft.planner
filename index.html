References & Learn

Learn the basics behind healthier daily decisions.

🥗 Nutrition

A balanced diet generally includes a variety of foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains and appropriate sources of protein and fats.

💧 Hydration

Your hydration needs depend on factors such as activity, climate, diet and individual circumstances.

🏃 Physical Activity

Regular physical activity supports overall health. Start at a level appropriate for you and increase gradually.

😴 Sleep

Consistent, adequate sleep is an important part of physical and mental wellbeing.

🍔 Highly Processed Foods

Eating highly processed or high-sugar foods sometimes is not a failure. The goal is awareness and overall balance rather than perfection.

💊 Medication Safety

Take medicines according to the instructions provided by your healthcare professional or medication label. Do not change doses without professional advice.
